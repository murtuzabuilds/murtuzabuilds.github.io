Research & Vision

Interfaces taught people to operate computers. Intelligence will operate for them.

My research started with how interfaces shape decisions. My vision is AI that acts for people and stays accountable to them.

Human does the workAI does the work
01 · HCIDirect manipulationHuman operates. System waits.
02 · HAICollaborative intelligenceHuman asks. Model answers.
03 · AgenticOrchestrated autonomyHuman sets intent. Agents plan and act.

What I believe

  1. Intent is the new interface. People will describe outcomes, not steps.
  2. Autonomy is earned. Trust is built per task, and measured like retention.
  3. Every decision leaves a trace. If an agent acts, a person can see why.

What does Human-AI Interaction mean in practice?

Deciding where the AI acts alone, where it asks first, and where it steps aside. Then designing the signals that keep a person informed and in control, like confidence, sources and an easy undo. The assistant on this page is a small example: it shows its confidence and its source, and it says so when it doesn't know.
