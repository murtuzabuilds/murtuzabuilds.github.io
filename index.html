Flagship 1 of 2 · Governance

Umbra.
Using AI to govern AI.

A product I designed and built from scratch. Umbra shows a company every AI tool and agent its people use, catches sensitive data before it goes out, and turns the unapproved tools it finds into a plan for where to invest in AI next. AI helps draft the rules, but fixed rules and people make the decisions.

Sample company: Kestrel Mutual, a fictional insurer with 2,400 people.

Numbers from a seeded simulation with made-up work. They show how the rules behave, not how a company would perform.

The GateMoney over a bot's limit, anything sent outside and anything that cannot be undone wait for a person's yes.
The VaultOff-limits data. A bot that reaches outside its job is stopped before the tool runs. You keep it out, allow it once, or pause it.
TrustGrows with each task shipped and shows on the bot. Each level raises what it may do alone. One click takes it away.
Autonomy labChange a limit and replay 20 shifts before it is applied. You see what it buys and what it costs.
LogbookEvery decision, the rule that fired and who made it. It checks itself and exports as JSON.
One decision pointA single tested function answers allow, hold or stop. No model decides anything.

Research & Vision

Interfaces taught people to operate computers. Intelligence will operate for them.

My research started with how interfaces shape decisions. My vision is AI that acts for people and stays accountable to them.

Human does the workAI does the work
01 · HCIDirect manipulationHuman operates. System waits.
02 · HAICollaborative intelligenceHuman asks. Model answers.
03 · AgenticOrchestrated autonomyHuman sets intent. Agents plan and act.

What I believe

  1. Intent is the new interface. People will describe outcomes, not steps.
  2. Autonomy is earned. Trust is built per task, and measured like retention.
  3. Every decision leaves a trace. If an agent acts, a person can see why.

What does Human-AI Interaction mean in practice?

Deciding where the AI acts alone, where it asks first, and where it steps aside. Then designing the signals that keep a person informed and in control, like confidence, sources and an easy undo. The assistant on this page is a small example: it shows its confidence and its source, and it says so when it doesn't know.
